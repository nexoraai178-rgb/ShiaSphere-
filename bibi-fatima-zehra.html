🌹 Bibi Fatima Zahra (SA)

Daughter of Prophet Muhammad (SAWW)

Introduction

Bibi Fatima Zahra (SA) Prophet Muhammad (SAWW) aur Hazrat Khadija (SA) ki beti thin. Unka nikah Imam Ali (AS) se hua aur woh Imam Hasan (AS) aur Imam Hussain (AS) ki walida hain. 0

Shia Islam me unhe taqwa, sabr aur ibadat ki misaal mana jata hai. Unka laqab "Al-Zahra" mashhoor hai. 1

Unki zindagi musalmanon ke liye akhlaq, haya aur Allah ki ita'at ka behtareen namuna hai. 2
