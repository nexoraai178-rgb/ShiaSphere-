Introduction

Imam Hussain ibn Ali (AS), Imam Ali (AS) aur Bibi Fatima Zahra (SA) ke bete aur Prophet Muhammad (SAWW) ke nawase the.

Shia Islam ke mutabiq woh teesre Imam hain.

10 Muharram 61 Hijri ko Karbala me unhone haq aur insaf ke liye qurbani di.

Karbala ka paigham sabr, himmat aur zulm ke khilaf khade hone ki taleem deta hai.
