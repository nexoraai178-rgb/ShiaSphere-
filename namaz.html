🧼 Wuzu

  1. Niyyat for Wuzu.
  2. Wash the face.
  3. Wash the right arm, then the left arm.
  4. Perform Masah of the front part of the head.
  5. Perform Masah of the right foot, then the left foot.

For the exact religious wording and detailed rulings, ShiaSphere should use a verified marja/source text rather than inventing or paraphrasing religious text.

🕋 Five Daily Prayers

Fajr — 2 Rak'at

Two-rak'at morning prayer.

Dhuhr — 4 Rak'at

Four-rak'at noon prayer.

Asr — 4 Rak'at

Four-rak'at afternoon prayer.

Maghrib — 3 Rak'at

Three-rak'at evening prayer.

Isha — 4 Rak'at

Four-rak'at night prayer.

📖 Basic Prayer Flow

  1. Niyyat.
  2. Takbirat-ul-Ihram.
  3. Qiyam and recitation.
  4. Ruku.
  5. Two Sajdahs.
  6. Continue the required rak'ahs.
  7. Tashahhud and Salam where required.
